Rights of members

Your voice and opportunity matter.

  • Vote and be voted.
  • Share in dividends, patronage refunds, benefits, and cooperative opportunities.
  • Access financial and developmental programs.
  • Attend and speak at General Assemblies.

Member benefits

More ways to thrive.

Financial advantages

  • Tax-free interest on share capital and patronage refunds
  • Membership options for Senior, Regular, Kiddie, and Group members
  • Interest on savings and time deposits
  • Higher interest rates on savings, share capital, and patronage refunds
  • Bonuses and incentives

Referral rewards

  • DRC: Direct Referral Commission — ₱500 per member
  • IDRC: Indirect Referral Commission — ₱300 per member
  • Consumer sales commission for personal and non-member sales
  • Affiliate business partner referral commission for personal and non-member referrals

Support for every stage

  • Scholarship grants for qualified member dependents
  • Mortuary benefit
  • Trainings and seminars
  • Standard membership encouraged through the sofa coin bank option
