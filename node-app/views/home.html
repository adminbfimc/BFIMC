Who is BFI MPC?

A cooperative built for every family’s future.

Beatitudes Family International Multipurpose Cooperative (BFIMPC) is a member-owned cooperative registered with the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA): Registration No: 9520-1015000000044320.

  • Accessible financial opportunities for members and their families.
  • A culture of cooperation that creates shared progress.
  • A secure, sustainable future built together.

We exist to uplift the lives of our members through meaningful opportunities and a shared commitment to a stronger tomorrow.

Why choose BFI MPC?

Opportunity with a purpose.

We strengthen the foundation of society—the family—through practical solutions that improve every member’s economic and social well-being.

Primary objective

Build stronger families, together.

Deliver solutions that create positive change and strengthen the family as the foundation of society.

Secondary objectives

  • Encourage consistent, regular saving.
  • Grow family unity through collective ownership.
  • Create sustainable opportunities for employment, education, and training.
  • Support intergenerational wealth-building and economic resilience.
  • Promote innovation and youth empowerment.

BFI MPC history

From a shared beginning to a growing cooperative.

A journey shaped by friendship, family, and a commitment to move forward together.

January 2018

A cooperative idea takes root.

Fifteen high school batchmates from Fort Del Pilar High School Batch ’95 and their families formed BFI MPC with pooled start-up funds of ₱280,000 for its credit operation capital.

September 10, 2018

Officially CDA registered.

Registered as Beatitudes Family International Credit Cooperative, with its office in Poblacion, Bontoc, Mountain Province. Its community grew to include members across the Philippines and Filipinos living overseas.

2019

A new home in Baguio.

The office moved to GP Arcade on Mabini Street, Baguio City.

2023

Continuing to grow.

BFI MPC transferred to 96D Purok 6, Lower Liwanag, Loakan, Baguio City.

2024

Expanding what we offer.

The cooperative became multipurpose, expanding into consumer products and affiliate business partnerships.
